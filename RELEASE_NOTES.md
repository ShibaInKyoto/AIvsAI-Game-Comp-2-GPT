A standalone native 3D game for Windows 10/11 (64-bit), with a Linux build included.

You owe the conductor £600. Explore an impossible night train, pack strange freight and bring it back before dawn. Turn the timetable to rearrange complete carriages, awaken resonance bonuses, or sound the choir organ to draw the conductor away.

Four travelers offer sealed fares for your tools. Reach dawn after helping them to remember their first mornings in the porter's notebook. Random and daily tickets, delivery upgrades and escalating endless services offer different routes through the night. Original modeled art, carriage soundscapes and a resolved dawn score accompany the journey.

Download **TheMidnightExchange-Windows.zip**, extract it and open **TheMidnightExchange.exe**. No engine installation or network connection is needed. Controls and strategies are included in READ_ME.md.

This release is built by GitHub Actions after simulation, physics/gameplay integration, and packaged Windows runtime checks. SHA-256 hashes accompany the ZIPs.
